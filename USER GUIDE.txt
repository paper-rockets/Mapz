CASE BRAIN — QUICK USER GUIDE
=============================

1. OPEN THE APP
Double-click "Start Server.cmd".
Keep the black window open while using Case Brain.
Open the address shown in that window, usually:
http://localhost:3008/traceline/

1b. SEE THE DEMO FIRST (OPTIONAL)
Click the case name at the top, then "Open the demo case".
Press the gold play button at the bottom to watch the money move
from 2023 to 2026. Tap any card to see who it is linked to.

2. ADD CASE DATA (WITH CHATGPT)
Click "Import data" at the top.
Click "Copy instructions".
In your work ChatGPT: paste the instructions, paste all your case notes
underneath, and send.
ChatGPT answers with four short lists: PEOPLE, ACCOUNTS, PAYMENTS
and NOTES. In PAYMENTS it can just use names, e.g. Jane Smith.
Copy ChatGPT's whole answer (its copy button is easiest) and paste it into
the big box in Case Brain.
Check the counts, then click "Add to case".

Headings, tables and extra chat text in the answer are fine.
If a few lines can't be read, the app adds everything else and lists
the ones it left out, with the reason. Pasting again never adds anything twice.

You can also add or edit individual people, accounts and payments using
the tabs on the left.

3. EXPLORE THE CASE
Click a person, account, payment or finding to see more detail.
Drag the map to move around.
Use + and - to zoom, or use the fit-to-screen button.
Use the tabs on the right for Findings, Review, Questions and Dossier.

4. PRESENT OR EXPORT
Click "Present" for a full-screen case briefing.
Click "Export" to save a backup, image or PowerPoint presentation.

5. FINISH
Case Brain saves changes automatically in this browser on this computer.
Close the browser tab when finished.
Close the black server window to stop the app.

IMPORTANT
Use approved test or case data according to your organization's policies.
Do not enter PINs, passwords, CVVs, authentication codes or complete card numbers.
